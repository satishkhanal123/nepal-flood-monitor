Operations & access

21,000+ security personnel deployed

Search, rescue and relief continue on day 9. Private operators added 129 emergency helicopter flights.

3,950 people in temporary centres

Shelters reported in Nuwakot, Dhading, Rasuwa and Kathmandu. Relief still moves by road and helicopter where bridges are gone.

Langtang Valley remains cut off

Roads and the Syabrubesi bridge to Dhunche were swept away. Locals have food for roughly 4–6 weeks; later resupply depends on helicopters.

Mobile service ~95% restored, quality uneven

Nepal Telecom reports flood-hit towers back; Ncell still rebuilding three destroyed sites. Microwave and 30 satellite phones fill the gaps.
